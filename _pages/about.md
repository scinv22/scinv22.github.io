I am a joint PhD candidate in the cotutelle doctoral program between the [Academy of Scientific and Innovative Research (AcSIR)](https://acsir.res.in/) and [RMIT University](https://www.rmit.edu.au/), Melbourne &mdash; a program I've been part of since May 2026, jointly supervised by [Dr. Rajesh P. Barnwal](https://www.cmeri.res.in/user-details/1284) at CSIR-CMERI and [Dr. Haytham Fayek](https://haythamfayek.com/) and [Prof. Feng Xia](https://www.xia.ai/) at RMIT. I'm currently based at the AI & IoT Lab of [CSIR&ndash;Central Mechanical Engineering Research Institute (CSIR-CMERI)](https://www.cmeri.res.in/), Durgapur, and will relocate to RMIT on 1 February 2027 to continue the program directly under my supervisors there.

My research sits at the intersection of computer vision and autonomous systems: teaching machines to *understand* road scenes rather than just detect objects in them &mdash; recognising events, reasoning about how agents relate to one another, and inferring cause and effect in the ambiguous, everyday traffic that self-driving cars actually have to navigate. The gap between a model that spots a pedestrian and one that grasps *why* they just stepped off the curb is where most of the interesting work lives, and it's the problem I keep coming back to.

This path started with a B.Tech project at [Jalpaiguri Government Engineering College](https://jgec.ac.in/) and an internship at [IIT Kharagpur](https://www.iitkgp.ac.in/) exploring distributed storage systems and coding theory &mdash; a detour through systems and information theory that still shapes how I think about structure and reasoning today.
