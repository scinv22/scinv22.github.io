I am a joint PhD candidate at the [Academy of Scientific and Innovative Research (AcSIR)](https://acsir.res.in/) and the [Royal Melbourne Institute of Technology (RMIT University)](https://www.rmit.edu.au/), Melbourne. I am jointly supervised by [Dr. Rajesh P. Barnwal](https://www.cmeri.res.in/user-details/1284) at AcSIR, CSIR-CMERI, and [Dr. Haytham Fayek](https://haythamfayek.com/) and [Prof. Feng Xia](https://www.xia.ai/) at RMIT University. I'm currently based at the AI & IoT Lab of [CSIR&ndash;Central Mechanical Engineering Research Institute (CSIR-CMERI)](https://www.cmeri.res.in/), Durgapur, and will relocate to RMIT University in February 2027 to continue the program under my supervisors there.

My research sits at the intersection of computer vision and autonomous systems: teaching machines to *understand* road scenes rather than just perceive objects in them &mdash; recognising events, reasoning about how agents relate to one another, and inferring cause and effect in the ambiguous, everyday traffic that self-driving cars actually have to navigate. The gap between a model that spots a car in front and one that grasps *why* it just slowed down is where most of the interesting work lives, and it's the problem I keep coming back to.

This journey started with a B.Tech project at [Jalpaiguri Government Engineering College](https://jgec.ac.in/) and an internship at [Indian Institute of Technology, Kharagpur](https://www.iitkgp.ac.in/) exploring distributed storage systems and coding theory &mdash; a beautiful detour through systems and information theory that still shapes how I think about information, structure, and reasoning today.
