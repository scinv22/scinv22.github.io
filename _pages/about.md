I am a joint PhD candidate (cotutelle) at the [Academy of Scientific and Innovative Research (AcSIR)](https://acsir.res.in/) and [Royal Melbourne Institute of Technology (RMIT University)](https://www.rmit.edu.au/), working with [Dr. Rajesh P. Barnwal](https://www.cmeri.res.in/user-details/1284) at AcSIR and [Dr. Haytham Fayek](https://haythamfayek.com/) and [Prof. Feng Xia](https://www.xia.ai/) at RMIT. I am currently based at the AI & IoT Lab of [CSIR&ndash;Central Mechanical Engineering Research Institute (CSIR-CMERI)](https://www.cmeri.res.in/) in Durgapur, and will move to Melbourne in February 2027 to continue my PhD at RMIT.

My research is at the intersection of computer vision and autonomous systems, with a focus on event recognition, scene understanding, and causal reasoning. More broadly, I am interested in how machines can move beyond recognising objects to understanding what is happening in a scene, how agents interact, and why things happen.

My research journey began with an internship at the [Indian Institute of Technology, Kharagpur](https://www.iitkgp.ac.in/), followed by my B.Tech project at [Jalpaiguri Government Engineering College](https://jgec.ac.in/), both in distributed storage systems and coding theory. Those early experiences continue to shape how I think about information, structure, and reasoning.
